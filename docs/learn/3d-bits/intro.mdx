# 3D Bits

Your shopper cannot pick up your product, turn it over, or ask you what the oak version looks like next to the walnut one. 3D Bits gives them the next best thing. They spin the product, change the material, drag the width to the size they actually need, and watch it update in front of them. When they add it to the cart, they are charged for exactly the thing they built.

## What you get

Everything you need to build and run a configurator is in the app.

You build in **Composer**, our visual editor. Upload your 3D model, arrange the scene, and design the panel of options your shoppers will use: dropdowns, colour swatches, sliders, text fields for personalisation, image uploads. You decide what each choice does to the model and what it adds to the price. There is no code involved, and you can press Play at any moment to try the configurator as a shopper will see it.

You set your **prices** in the same place. A starting price, an amount per option, and a formula over a slider for anything sold by size or length. On the Pro plan you can also attach products from your own catalogue to particular choices, and build a **parts** list behind the configuration, so every order arrives with the bill of materials your workshop needs. The total updates live as the shopper chooses, and Shopify charges that amount at checkout.

You put it on your store with a **single switch**. Turn on the 3D Bits app embed once in your theme, and every product you publish from the app shows its configurator automatically. No theme editing per product, and nothing to paste into templates.

If you make things that are genuinely parametric, where the shape itself is calculated rather than swapped between prepared models, the Standard and Pro plans let you drive the scene with **scripts** built in our visual node editor or written in TypeScript.

## It works with the store you already have

3D Bits does not ask you to rebuild your product pages. It slots into the theme you have, next to the buy button you already use, and it respects the way your store is set up.

If Shopify variants already describe your product well, keep them. The configurator will follow them and show the right model when someone picks a colour or a size. If you run a product options app you are happy with, keep that too, and simply leave configurator pricing switched off for those products. You can mix both approaches across your catalogue, product by product.

Shopify keeps doing what Shopify does. Shipping, discounts, payment and refunds are unchanged, and we never touch your money. 3D Bits decides what the configured product costs, and Shopify collects it.

Tax is worth one look before you go live. With the default Product lines charging, part of the configured price is carried by helper products 3D Bits generates in your catalogue, and Shopify taxes those at your shop's default rate. If you rely on a reduced or zero rate set up as a tax override, it will not necessarily reach them, so check it before you go live - [Helper products](/learn/3d-bits/pricing/helper-products) explains what these generated products are.

## Getting started

[Quick Start](/learn/3d-bits/quick-start) walks you through installing the app, switching 3D on for your store, and getting your first product live.

Most of the effort goes into preparing a good 3D model, and that is worth doing properly. A well-made model loads fast, looks like your product, and behaves predictably when parts are swapped. Our [3D Assets](/learn/3d-bits/3d-assets) guides cover how to get there, and we prepare models for merchants as a paid service if you would rather hand it over.

If you want to understand what happens on the product page before you commit, [How It Works](/learn/3d-bits/how-it-works) explains it.

## Plans

There are three plans: **Base**, **Standard** and **Pro**.

Base covers the core of it. You build configurators in Composer, use unlimited 3D models across unlimited products, and price and charge what your shoppers configure.

Standard adds the features that help you tell the story of a product. Points of interest that fly the camera to a detail worth showing, measurement annotations, gradient and image backgrounds, and scripting for parametric products.

Pro is for products that ask the most of the tool. Printed and engraved personalisation applied directly onto the model, options linked to real products in your catalogue, a parts list that turns each configuration into a bill of materials, and PDF documents generated for your shopper and for the people who make the thing.

See [Subscription Plans](/learn/3d-bits/plans/subscription-plans) for the full comparison and current prices.
