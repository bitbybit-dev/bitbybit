# GUI Elements at a Glance

The option panel beside your 3D model is built from three kinds of pieces. **Controls** are what the shopper sets, **ways to show a choice** decide how a list of options looks, and **elements** arrange the panel and show everything around the controls. Below is each of them, as the panel draws it on the default theme; [Style and Translations](./style-and-translations.md) changes how they look, [Layout](./layout.md) where they go, and [Controls](./controls.md) explains what every control can do.

## Controls {#controls}

A control is what the shopper sets: each one has a key, and its value drives the model, the price and the order.

### Dropdown {#dropdown}

One choice from a list that opens on a click. It takes the least room, so it suits long lists.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/dropdown */}

### Radio {#radio}

One choice, every option in view. Drawn as plain text by default, or in any of the displays below.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/radio */}

### Checkbox {#checkbox}

Any number of choices from a list, each with its own price when it has one.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/checkbox */}

### Switch {#switch}

On or off, for one feature a shopper adds or leaves out.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/switch */}

### Range {#range}

A number picked with a slider, between a minimum and a maximum, in steps.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/range */}

### Text {#text}

A line of the shopper's own words, such as a name to print or engrave.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/text */}

### Number {#number}

A number typed or stepped with the arrows, between a minimum and a maximum.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/number */}

### Colour {#color}

Any colour the shopper picks, for a part that can be painted freely.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/color */}

### Date {#date}

A day picked from a calendar, such as a delivery date.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/date */}

### Time {#time}

A time of day, such as a pick-up slot.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/time */}

### Email {#email}

An email address, checked as the shopper types it.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/email */}

### File {#file}

A file the shopper uploads, such as their own artwork for a decal.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/file */}

### Text area {#textarea}

Several lines of text, for notes or longer wording.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/textarea */}

### Button {#button}

A button that runs an action, such as showing a part up close or resetting the view.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/button */}

## Elements {#elements}

Elements arrange the panel and show everything around the controls: groups, text, pictures, the price and the buttons that buy.

### Section {#section}

A group with a heading and an optional description; what it holds is laid out one under the other.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/section */}

### Row {#row}

Lays what it holds side by side, wrapping onto a new line when there is no room.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/row */}

### Accordion {#accordion}

A group that opens and closes on its title, so a long panel stays short.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/accordion */}

### Tabs {#tabs}

Several groups under one strip of tabs, one shown at a time.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/tabs */}

### Message {#message}

A notice in one of four colours: information, a warning, a success or an error.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/message */}

### Text {#text-block}

Words of your own, as a heading, a subheading, body text or a caption. Write a link as the label in square brackets followed by the address in round ones.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/text-block */}

### Image {#image}

A picture with an optional caption.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/image */}

### Divider {#divider}

A line, or empty space, between two parts of the panel.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/divider */}

### Parts list {#parts-list}

What the configured product is made of, part by part with quantities, live as the shopper chooses.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/parts-list */}

### Price {#price}

The price of the configuration as it stands, with its breakdown if you show one.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/price */}

### Quantity {#quantity}

How many of the configured product the shopper buys.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/quantity */}

### Add to cart {#add-to-cart}

The button that puts the configured product in the cart, with its configuration.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/add-to-cart */}

### Confirmation {#confirmation}

What the shopper sees after adding to the cart: a heading and a message of your own, in place of the panel or under it. It shows on the storefront once something is added, so it has no picture here.

### Validation summary {#validation-summary}

Every choice still missing or wrong, each a link to its control.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/validation-summary */}

### Download PDF {#download-pdf}

A button that downloads a summary of the configuration as a PDF.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/download-pdf */}

### Link {#link}

A link to another page, as text or as a button.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/link */}

### Custom HTML {#html}

Markup of your own, cleaned of anything that could run, with its own styles.

{/* shot: composer.gui.elements/html */}

Buttons and sliders that zoom and turn the view, for shoppers who would rather not drag.
