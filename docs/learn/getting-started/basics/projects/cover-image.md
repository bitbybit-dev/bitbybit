# Using Cover Images for Your Projects and Scripts

## Why a cover matters

A cover image is what people see first: on the community projects, on the project's own page beside its scripts, in search results and in the preview a link shows on social media. A project needs one before it can be shared at all: until it has a cover, <Ui>Private</Ui> is its only visibility.

Scripts can have covers of their own. Without one, a script shows a placeholder.

## Add a cover

{/* shot: platform.projects.cover/form */}

1. Take a picture of your work: a screenshot of the model works well (<kbd>Cmd+Shift+4</kbd> on macOS, the Snipping Tool on Windows).
2. In [My Projects](https://bitbybit.dev/projects/my), choose the cloud project.
3. Choose <Ui>Cover image</Ui> for the project, or the image icon on a script's row for that script.
4. Pick an image of at least 960 by 960 pixels: PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, BMP or AVIF.
5. Crop it to the square that shows best and choose <Ui>Accept</Ui>.

bitbybit.dev keeps the cover in three sizes, so it stays sharp in the gallery and quick to load. Covers are for cloud projects: a project in this browser has none.
