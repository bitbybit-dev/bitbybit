# A Parametric Cube

Parametric means that a shape follows its parameters: change a number and the geometry is rebuilt from it. In Rete a parameter is just another component whose output is wired into an input. Here a number slider sizes a cube made by OCCT, the CAD kernel behind solids on Bitbybit.

## Before you start

Open [an empty Rete editor](https://bitbybit.dev/app?editor=rete). If this is your first Rete script, [Hello World](./hello-world.mdx) shows the basics first.

## The steps

{/* steps:start - generated from the tutorial's steps, which the capture plays in the editor; edits between these markers are overwritten */}

1. **Add cube from the palette.** Open <Ui>occt</Ui> > <Ui>shapes</Ui> > <Ui>solid</Ui> along the top of the canvas and click <Ui>cube</Ui> under primitives.

   The palette follows the API: occt, then shapes, then solid. Cube sits among the primitives. Its path, occt.shapes.solid.createCube, is printed on the node and links to its documentation.

   <Shot id="rete.tutorials.parametric-cube/cube" />

2. **Run the script.** Press <Ui>Run The Code</Ui>, the play button at the lower left.

   Run the script once. The cube is a solid made by OCCT, the CAD kernel, and Rete draws it for you.

   <Shot id="rete.tutorials.parametric-cube/run" />

3. **Add number slider from the palette.** Open <Ui>math</Ui> along the top of the canvas and click <Ui>number slider</Ui> under create.

   A number slider gives a value you can drag. It starts at 5, between 0 and 10.

   <Shot id="rete.tutorials.parametric-cube/slider" />

4. **Connect number slider to the size of cube.** Drag from the output on the right of number slider to the size input of cube.

   Wire the slider's output into the cube's size. The cube takes the slider's 5 at once.

   <Shot id="rete.tutorials.parametric-cube/connected" />

5. **Set number to `2.5`** on number slider.

   Drag the slider, or focus it and use the arrow keys: the cube follows every value, because its size now comes from the slider.

   <Shot id="rete.tutorials.parametric-cube/smaller" />

## What you learned

- **The palette follows the API.** A component's path on the node, such as `occt.shapes.solid.createCube`, is where it sits in the palette and what you call from [TypeScript](../../typescript/intro.md).
- **A wire makes a parameter.** Any output that fits can drive an input; the editor refuses a wire that does not fit and says why ([Nodes, wires and values](../features/nodes-and-wires.md)).
- **Sliders have limits.** Open the slider's options (⌄) to change its minimum, maximum and step.
