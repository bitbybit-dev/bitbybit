# Share a design

## Share it on bitbybit.dev

1. Keep the project in the cloud: a project in this browser cannot be shared.
2. Give it a cover: in <Ui>My Projects</Ui>, choose the project, then <Ui>Cover image</Ui>. A project without one can only stay private.
3. Choose <Ui>Edit details</Ui> and set its <Ui>Visibility</Ui>: <Ui>Public</Ui> for everyone, or <Ui>Silver community</Ui> or <Ui>Gold community</Ui> for members of those plans and above.
4. Under <Ui>Visitors can download</Ui>, choose what people below Gold may download: <Ui>Nothing</Ui>, the default; <Ui>Model</Ui>, a GLB or STL to view or 3D print; or <Ui>Everything</Ui>, STEP for other CAD tools and the project file as well. Then <Ui>Save</Ui>.
5. Send the link of the script, as your browser shows it while the design is open.

<Shot id="designer.get-started.share/visibility" />

Who sees what:

- **A Gold member** who opens the link sees the design in the Designer, as it is. Their changes are kept as their own copy and never touch yours.
- **Anyone else**, signed in on another plan or not signed in, opens it in the Designer to view, marked <Ui>View only</Ui>. They can turn it, select its parts, read its tree, parts list and clashes, switch its configurations, and try the values you [let visitors change](../modelling/parameters.md#let-visitors-try-values). Everything that would change it is greyed out, and <Ui>Edit with Gold</Ui> shows the plans. They can download only what you chose under <Ui>Visitors can download</Ui>.

So a public Designer project can be viewed by everyone and changed, as a copy, by Gold members.

:::note What Nothing means
To show a design, bitbybit.dev sends its documents and shapes to the visitor's browser. <Ui>Nothing</Ui> takes the download buttons away; it does not hide the design from someone determined to copy it. To keep a design to yourself, keep its project private.
:::

## Share a file

Export the project with <Ui>File</Ui> > <Ui>Export...</Ui> > <Ui>Project</Ui> and send the file. It opens in the Designer with <Ui>File</Ui> > <Ui>Import design documents</Ui>, and a STEP or GLB export opens in most other CAD programs and viewers.

## Share a preview or embed it

A preview shows the design on its own, without the Designer around it: the model to turn and inspect, a choice of its configurations, the values you let visitors try, and <Ui>Open on bitbybit.dev</Ui>. It opens for anyone who can open the project and is view only for everyone, Gold members included.

<Shot id="designer.get-started.share/preview" />

1. With the design open, choose the share button at the left of the bitbybit.dev header (<Ui>Share script preview</Ui>), or <Ui>Share a preview</Ui> beside the script in <Ui>My Projects</Ui>.
2. Copy the preview's link, or choose <Ui>Embed Code</Ui> and paste it into your own page: [Embedding Scripts](/learn/getting-started/basics/scripts/embed) explains the rest.
