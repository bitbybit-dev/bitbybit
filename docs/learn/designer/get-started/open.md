# Open the Designer

The Designer runs in your browser on bitbybit.dev and is part of the Gold plan.

{/* shot: designer.get-started.open/apps-menu */}

## From the Apps menu

1. Open <Ui>Apps</Ui> in the bitbybit.dev header.
2. Choose <Ui>Designer</Ui>.

The Designer opens with an empty project. Nothing is saved until you make your first change; then your work is kept as a draft in this browser, in the <Ui>Drafts</Ui> project, and you can [move it to the cloud](./save.md#move-to-the-cloud) later.

## From the start dialog

Where bitbybit.dev asks which editor to start, press <Ui>Launch Designer</Ui> on the Designer's card. Below Gold the card offers <Ui>See the plans</Ui> instead. The homepage shows the same cards, under *Start creating now*.

## From your projects

Make a project whose first script is a Designer script, or add a Designer script to a project you have: [Create a Designer project](./create-project.md) shows both.

## Below the Gold plan

{/* shot: designer.get-started.open/gold-plan */}

The Designer is marked <Ui>Gold</Ui> wherever bitbybit.dev offers it. Below Gold, the start dialog and the project forms show it greyed out, with <Ui>See the plans</Ui> beside it.

A Designer project that someone shares with you still opens in the Designer, to view. <Ui>View only</Ui> at the top says so:

{/* shot: designer.get-started.open/view-only */}

- **It opens as its author last built it**, from the geometry kept with the project, so even a large design is quick to open.
- **Look at it as you like:** turn and zoom the view, select parts, open its documents, and read the tree, the parts list, the clashes and each part's mass.
- **Switch its configurations**, such as an arm's poses, and roll a part back through its features, to see how it was built.
- **Try other values** where the author lets visitors: drag a slider under <Ui>Try other values</Ui> and the design rebuilds in your browser. Nothing you try is saved.
- **Changing it is part of Gold.** Every command that would change the design is greyed out; point at one to see why, and <Ui>Edit with Gold</Ui> shows the plans.
- **Downloads** are what the author allows: nothing, the model as GLB or STL, or everything, STEP and the project file included. Your own Designer projects always offer everything.
