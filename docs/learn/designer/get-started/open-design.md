# Open a design again

<Shot id="designer.get-started.open-design/project" />

1. Open <Ui>My Projects</Ui> in the bitbybit.dev header.
2. On the left, choose the project: your cloud projects are listed first, then the ones in this browser. Drafts you started from the Apps menu are in <Ui>Drafts</Ui>, in this browser.
3. Choose <Ui>Open</Ui> beside the script.

The Designer opens the design with every one of its documents, listed at the top of the <Ui>Features</Ui> panel, where you switch between them.

## Someone else's design

A public design, or one shared with your plan's community, opens in the Designer as it is. On Gold, when you change something, your version is kept as a copy in this browser, named "Copy of" and the original's title. The original never changes. Below Gold it opens to view: [Open the Designer](./open.md#below-the-gold-plan) says what you can do there.
