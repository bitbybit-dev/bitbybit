# A family house in IFC

The [IFC page](/learn/code/common/ifc/intro) builds a wall with a window. This project builds a whole house with the same functions: a two-storey family house with some 700 elements, as an architect's tool would write it, under a sky whose sun follows a slider. Run it, move the slider through the day, then open it in the editor to read and change it.

## The files of the project

The script is a TypeScript project of several files, which the editor shows in its file tree: open it to read and change any of them. The house itself is the house of the IFC example for Vite and three.js, [`examples/vite/threejs/ifc-house`](https://github.com/bitbybit-dev/bitbybit/tree/master/examples/vite/threejs/ifc-house), file for file:

- **`house/house-types.ts`** - the shapes of the plan's data: a wall run, a window or door, a slab, a detail.
- **`house/house-plan.ts`** - the plan, all in metres: the materials and the layered build-ups of walls, floors and the roof, the window and door types, the wall runs of both storeys, every window and door, the slabs, the rooms and their property sets.
- **`house/details.ts`** - the details worked out from the plan: cladding boards around the openings, flashings, window surrounds and sills, copings, the stair, rainwater pipes, the entrance canopy, the carport frame, glass rails and the solar array.
- **`house/build-house.ts`** - `HouseAuthor`, which writes all of it into one model with `bitbybit.ifc`: storeys, materials, walls, slabs, windows and doors, the roof, the carport, the details, assemblies, the garden, rooms, properties and quantities. The console shows each stage as it starts.
- **`scene/draw-house.ts`** - the geometry: `ifc.geometry.recipe` describes the model as data, `manifold.recipes.build` builds every element as a mesh solid, which is fast for a model this size, and each element is drawn in the colour of its material, glass translucent.
- **`scene/environment.ts`** - the setting, in BabylonJS: a sky material whose sun follows the time of day, a sun light with soft shadows, the sky reflected in every material, ACES tone mapping, a light haze, and the slider at the bottom of the view that moves the sun from morning to evening.
- **`main.ts`** - runs the three in turn and places the camera.

To export the model as an IFC file, give `bitbybit.ifc.model.write` the model `HouseAuthor` returns; [IFC in the npm packages](/learn/npm-packages/ifc) shows the same house in an app of your own.
